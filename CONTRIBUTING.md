# Contributing to minou-sync

Thanks for your interest. minou-sync is a personal project maintained by [@andrewtpham](https://github.com/andrewtpham). It's open under the [MIT License](LICENSE) and contributions are welcome.

## Before you start

- For a bug or a small fix, open an issue or a pull request directly.
- For a larger change, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach before you spend time on it.
- Follow the patterns and style already in the code. `AGENTS.md` describes how the project is built and tested.
- Run the tests before you open a pull request: `.venv/bin/pytest -q`. They must pass. Never put real activity or health values in tests; make them up.

## How contributions are licensed

By contributing you agree to two things. Both exist so the project can stay open and still have a clear owner.

### 1. Developer Certificate of Origin

Sign off each commit with `git commit -s`. This adds a `Signed-off-by` line and means you agree to the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/): in short, that you wrote the contribution yourself, or have the right to submit it, and that you understand it will be distributed as part of a public, open-source project.

### 2. License grant

You keep the copyright to your contribution. You are not assigning it.

Alongside that, you grant Andrew Pham a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, and sublicense your contribution, and to relicense it under any terms, including proprietary or commercial terms.

In plain words: your contribution stays MIT in this repository, you still own it and may use it however you like, and this grant lets the maintainer license the project, including your contribution, under other terms in the future without having to ask each contributor. If you don't agree to this, please don't submit a contribution.
